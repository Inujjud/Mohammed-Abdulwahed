About the Author

Preserving the Soul of a Civilization

"A personal initiative aimed at introducing and disseminating our rich heritage and history, preserving our cultural legacy, and highlighting our authentic identity for future generations."

Mohammed Abdulwahed is an independent researcher and writer specializing in the fields of culture, history, and heritage in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Through his writings, he seeks to document the heritage, social, and historical aspects that prevailed in the past, relying on research, reading, and linking oral traditions with available historical sources.

This contributes to preserving local memory and highlighting the heritage identity of Saudi society. He has authored several books covering diverse topics in history, heritage, and genealogy. This website makes these works available free of charge to support the dissemination and sharing of knowledge.

He also welcomes communication with serious researchers and interested parties, encouraging them to share knowledge and ideas, believing in the importance of knowledge exchange and promoting scientific research in this field.
